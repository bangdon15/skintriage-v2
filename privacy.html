Privacy Policy & HIPAA Practices

Last Updated: October 2026 · Skin Triage LLC

1. Our Commitment to Patient Privacy

At Skin Triage LLC ("Skin Triage", "we", "us", or "our"), safeguarding your personal health information is our highest institutional priority. This Privacy Policy details our procedures regarding the collection, transmission, storage, and protection of health information, clinical images, and personal identifiers submitted through our telehealth triage platform.

2. HIPAA Compliance & Protected Health Information (PHI)

Skin Triage operates in full compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 ("HIPAA"), the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health ("HITECH") Act, and applicable state medical privacy laws. We maintain rigorous administrative, technical, and physical safeguards:

  • End-to-End Encryption: All health data and clinical photography are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256 bit military-grade encryption.
  • Role-Based Access Controls: Your screening data is accessible strictly to authorized state-licensed healthcare clinicians assigned to your case.
  • Zero Data Monetization: We never sell, rent, license, or barter your health data, photographs, or personal identity to advertisers, data brokers, or third-party marketers.

3. Information We Collect

To facilitate accurate dermatological screening triage, we collect:

  • Contact information (first and last name, email address, residential state).
  • Photographic images of concerning skin lesions, moles, or cutaneous abnormalities.
  • Clinical history and symptoms (duration, bleeding, itching, changes in pigmentation, sun exposure history).

4. Disclosures to Independent Licensed Providers

Skin Triage facilitates the secure transfer of your submitted intake data to independent, board-certified or licensed healthcare professionals licensed in your state for the sole purpose of clinical evaluation and triage guidance.

5. Your Rights and Inquiries

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal records in accordance with HIPAA standards. To exercise any privacy rights, contact our Privacy Officer directly at privacy@skintriage.org.
