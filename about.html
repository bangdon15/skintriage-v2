Epidemiological Evidence

Stage Determines Survival

When melanoma is found early (localized to the skin), the 5-year survival rate is dramatically higher than when diagnostic delays allow it to spread.

~100% 5-Year Survival Rate When diagnosed at localized stage
~34.6% 5-Year Survival Rate When diagnosis is delayed to distant spread

Official Clinical Source: National Cancer Institute SEER (Surveillance, Epidemiology, and End Results) database (2015–2021).

Overcoming Healthcare Inertia

Why Early Triage Matters

Too often, people postpone evaluation because they are unsure, intimidated by specialist waitlists, or fearful of medical costs.

Uncertainty leads to waiting. Waiting limits clinical options.

Currently, approximately 77% of melanomas are diagnosed at a localized stage where outcomes are best. Our core mission at Skin Triage is to push that number even higher by eliminating the friction and delay between noticing a spot and receiving licensed clinical feedback.
